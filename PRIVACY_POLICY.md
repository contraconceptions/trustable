# Privacy Policy for GamerRating Bot

**Effective Date:** August 12, 2025

## 1. Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Discord User ID**: Unique identifier provided by Discord
- **Discord Username**: Your current Discord username
- **Server Information**: Discord server IDs where the bot is present
- **Rating Data**: Ratings you give and receive (1-3 stars and optional comments)
- **Timestamps**: When ratings are submitted

### Information You Provide
- **Rating Comments**: Optional comments when rating other users
- **Bot Commands**: Commands you use to interact with the bot

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide cross-server reputation tracking
- Display rating statistics and profiles
- Prevent spam and abuse through rate limiting
- Generate leaderboards and badges
- Maintain system functionality and security

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:
- **Discord Integration**: Basic information shared through Discord's API
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect rights and safety
- **Service Providers**: Technical infrastructure providers under strict confidentiality

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored in secure PostgreSQL databases
- We implement industry-standard security measures
- Access is restricted to authorized personnel only
- Regular security audits and updates are performed

## 5. Data Retention

- Rating data is retained to maintain reputation system integrity
- Inactive user data may be archived after 2 years of inactivity
- You may request data deletion (see Your Rights section)

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your stored data
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (may affect reputation system)
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a structured format

To exercise these rights, contact us through our support channels.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Our bot integrates with:
- **Discord**: Subject to Discord's Privacy Policy
- **Neon Database**: Our database hosting provider
- **Replit**: Our hosting platform

## 9. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than your residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced in our support server. Continued use constitutes acceptance of updated terms.
